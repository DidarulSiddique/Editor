Three men were travelling when night fell, so they took shelter in a cave. Then a rock rolled down the mountain, and sealed it shut.

Trapped, they said: only our sincere deeds can save us now.

The first said: O Allah, I never fed my family before my old parents. One night they fell asleep, so I stood holding their milk until dawn. If that was for You, free us. The rock moved... a little.

The second said: I once had the chance to commit a great sin. But when I was reminded to fear You, I walked away. The rock moved again.

The third said: a worker left without his wage. I invested it, until it became a whole herd. When he came back, I gave him all of it.

The rock rolled away... and they walked out into the light.

Sincere deeds, done for Allah alone, open the way out.
