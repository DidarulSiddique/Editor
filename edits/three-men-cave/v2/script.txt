Three men were sealed inside a mountain... and the only key was a secret.

Imagine it's night. You and two friends duck into a cave. Then a rumble... and a huge rock slams over the entrance. Pitch black.

One of them says: only a deed done purely for Allah, that nobody saw, can move this rock.

But how can a secret move a rock?

The first man: his parents always drank before anyone. One night they fell asleep, so he stood holding their milk until dawn, while his own kids cried at his feet. The rock shifts. A crack of moonlight.

The second walked away from a great sin, with no one watching. The rock slides again... still too narrow.

The third had a worker who left without his pay. He invested it for years, until it was a whole herd. Then he handed the worker every single animal.

And the rock rolls clear.
