The most famous Egyptian queen in history... came from a Greek royal family.
Picture her. Golden collar. Cobra crown. All of Egypt bowing.
Now rewind almost three hundred years. Macedonia, north of Greece. A general named Ptolemy rides beside Alexander the Great.
Alexander dies young, and his empire gets carved up like a cake. Ptolemy grabs the richest slice. Egypt.
His family rules it for nearly three centuries, from Alexandria, speaking Greek.
Then Cleopatra the Seventh is born. And she does something no Ptolemy before her is known to have done.
She learns Egyptian.
She dresses as a pharaoh. She's hailed as the new Isis. She speaks to her people in their own language.
Born in Egypt? Yes. Ruler of Egypt? Absolutely. But her royal bloodline? Macedonian Greek.
She embraced Egypt so completely... history made her its most iconic pharaoh.
So which one was the real Cleopatra?
