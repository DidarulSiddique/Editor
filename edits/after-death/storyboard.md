# The First 7 Days After Death — storyboard (for approval)

**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 62 s, TikTok / Shorts / Reels.
**Feeling to leave with:** sobering, then hopeful. You can still help those who have passed, and you are writing your own "first 7 days" now.
**Sources rule:** only the Qur'an and sahih/hasan hadith. Anything without an authentic source is called out, not used.

## Script structure (from the story-arc reel)
- **Open at 70/100:** a contrarian hook in the first 5 s ("most of what you've heard has no source").
- **Spike to 90 by about 10 s:** the soul leaving the body.
- **Then peak and release:**
  - peak: footsteps
  - peak: the 3 questions
  - release: du'a
  - peak: mourning ends on day 3
  - release: what still reaches them
- **Final peak:** the turn onto the viewer.
- **CTA:** a comment keyword.

## Look (from the two animation references)
- **Visual system (reference 1):**
  - Dark plum background (#1a0a12) with a faint grid.
  - Flat vector shapes in maroon, cream (#ffe9b8) and soft blue/pink.
  - Faceless pill-shaped figures.
- **Type (reference 1):**
  - Bold sans text that builds in word by word with a blur-fade.
  - Key words in italic serif or on a cream highlight chip.
  - A small monospace source tag on every claim, e.g. `SAHIH AL-BUKHARI 1338`.
- **Story devices (reference 2):**
  - Hadith and Qur'an cards with Arabic, transliteration and English.
  - A one-line note at the start: *"Out of respect, no one is depicted."*
- **Transitions:** camera push-through a dark arch, a whip slide sideways, objects growing from small to full size, doors opening.
- **A 7-day strip** runs through the video, like reference 1's FRI…FRI bar: SUN … SAT tiles that light up as the story moves through the days.

## Audio
Reference 1 uses ambient music. Many viewers of Islamic content prefer no music, so I'd use **no music**: a soft ambient wind/room tone plus sound effects (stamp hit, whoosh, footsteps, page close, gentle chime on each source tag).

**Choose one for the voice:**
- **A. Text-only**, like reference 1. Free.
- **B. Voiceover** with a calm male English narrator through vidIQ. About 14 credits for this length (vidIQ charges 14 per 1,000 characters; you have 135). An English-speaking voice reading the Arabic phrases may mispronounce them.

## Notes for you
- I'm not a scholar. The hadith numbers above are standard references I'm confident in, but please check them, or have someone knowledgeable glance over the script, before posting.
- No prophets, angels or faces are drawn. Angels appear only as light, and people are faceless shapes.
- All visuals are drawn in code (vector shapes). That's the reference's own style, so no generated footage and no credits are needed for visuals.
