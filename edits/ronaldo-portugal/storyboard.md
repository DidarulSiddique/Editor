## Facts and sources (checked by search, 5 Nov 2026)
- **Debut and Euro 2004:**
  - Debut 20 Aug 2003 v Kazakhstan; #17 at Euro 2004.
  - Euro 2004 final lost 1–0 to Greece.
- **Captaincy and Euro 2016:**
  - Captain from 2008.
  - Hat-trick at Sweden in the Nov 2013 play-off.
  - Euro 2016: 3 goals; went off injured in the 25th minute of the final; Éder won it 1–0 aet.
- **Nations League and the record:**
  - 2019 Nations League: hat-trick v Switzerland in the semi-final; Portugal beat the Netherlands 1–0 in the final.
  - Record: passed Ali Daei with goals 110–111 v Ireland, 1 Sep 2021.
  - 2025 Nations League final: he scored the 2–2 equaliser v Spain, and Portugal won on penalties. He was 40.
- **2026 World Cup and totals:**
  - Scored twice v Uzbekistan (23 Jun 2026), the first man to score at 6 World Cups.
  - Portugal went out 1–0 to Spain in the round of 16.
  - 146 goals in 234 caps (UEFA, Squawka, LiveScore; about a month old).
