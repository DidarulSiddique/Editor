## Signature techniques (reusable)
1. **Image-matched type families** (2.8–14.1). Change the typeface per phrase to echo the shot's world: slab serif for vintage, pixel for sky/game, bevelled condensed for posters. Keep each for 1–3 words.
2. **Text living in the image** (9.5–10.9). Attach words to moving objects (word parented to the airplane) or anatomy (one letter per lens). Build it by tracking a null and parenting the text layer to it.
3. **Callback stutter + glitch** (15.6–16.5). Before a mood switch, cut 2–3-frame flashes of 3–4 earlier shots with their text, separated by 1-frame RGB glitch frames. Then hard-cut to a still, quiet shot.
4. **Giant translucent lyric over a slow section** (19.2–32). One word fills ~80–100% of frame height at ≈55% opacity and persists across 2–3 shot changes. Optionally roto the subject so they sit in front of it.
5. **Vowel-stretch scroll** (6.2–7.2). A held sung vowel becomes a row of repeated letters that scrolls horizontally for the full note length.
