## Signature techniques (reusable)
1. **Character-select HUD power-up** (8.4–9.4, 11.3–12.4, 13.6–15.1, 16.8–17.9 s): after a 1 s clean hold, on a beat (a) hue-shift the kit, (b) add 6–8 px neon stroke + glow to the roto matte, (c) slide in a dashed-box menu with 3–5 slanted buttons on the empty side; (d) step the highlight every 4–5 frames, decelerating; (e) cut to the chosen variant on the downbeat. Suits "player as X" concepts (e.g., player as each position/role, or rival comparisons).
2. **Pose-matched swap**: generate/source an alternate (AI art, comic, card) in the identical pose and framing; hard cut on the hit so it reads as transformation.
3. **Colour-coded outline per variant**: the stroke colour predicts the hero (purple Panther, red/blue Spider-Man, gold Thor, blue-purple Hawkeye).
4. **Smear whip exit** (2–4 frames radial/directional blur with a colour cast) to leave each payoff.
5. **Shape-iris inserts** (circle 25.0 s, star 29.4 s) to punch into a face mid-montage.
