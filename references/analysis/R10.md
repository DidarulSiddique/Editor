## Signature techniques (reusable)
1. **Per-scene matched lyric type**: pick a face that echoes each artwork (bubble letters, Cooper-like serif, ransom-note). Build as one text layer per word with letter-by-letter reveal at 2–3 f per letter.
2. **Text wall behind subject** (5.6–8.4): repeat a lyric line 5–6× as a stacked, semi-transparent block between background and subject cut-out; put the hero lyric in front of the subject.
3. **Beat zoom-blur pulse** (2.3, 4.1, 4.55, 7.2): 2–3 f scale up 5–10 % + gaussian/defocus, snap back. Cheap way to mark hits without cutting.
4. **Material transitions**: crumple, tear, shatter — collage-native transitions that hide scene changes (0.95, 10.4, 8.6).
5. **Metadata footer**: small monospace caption "Album, Year" bottom-left on each scene; gives a documentary/era structure.

## Weaknesses / don't copy
- Low resolution (576²) and 20 fps — stuttery letter animation and soft detail.
- Inconsistent typographic system (by design, but some faces feel arbitrary, e.g., "BETTER THAN EVER").
- No ending payoff; ~4 s platform outro dominates the tail.
- Blur pulses occasionally smear the subject so it is unreadable (4.1, 7.2).
