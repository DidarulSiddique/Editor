## Signature techniques (reusable)
1. **Quiet-quote → drop contrast** (0–7.0). Hold 2–3 static shots with sparse word-by-word captions opposite the face, then cut on the drop to a fully designed graphic world.
2. **Halftone duotone cut-out poster** (8.9–9.65). Layers from back to front:
   - Paper-coloured solid.
   - Rounded panel.
   - Huge condensed word.
   - Subject cut-out with a halftone dot filter in the same hue.
   - Micro-typography (header labels, body columns, credit footer).
   Slide the whole composition in with motion blur.
3. **Graphic-becomes-object continuity** (11.0, 20.5). Carry the previous scene's text card into the next shot as a held or physical object (chopsticks gripping the card; a 3D box labelled with the lyric word).
4. **Repeated-word stack** (9.7–10.4). 4 rows of one word alternating outline/fill, each row sliding in an opposite direction, with the translucent subject over the top.
5. **Glowing silhouette in hyperspace** (15.7–17.5). Fill the subject's matte with a flat glowing colour over light streaks. Put the word behind it with a scanline/RGB jitter, then tilt the whole plane out in 3D.
6. **Lyric-driven colour blocks**: each lyric phrase gets one dominant hue across all its layers.
