## Signature techniques (reusable)
1. **Colour-alternating word-per-shot run** (13.6–15.5). Cut every ≈0.35–0.4 s, one word each, alternating red and blue, set bottom-centre over the chest. Use it for a rapid lyric line or a stat list.
2. **Shape-masked footage that tells the story** (21.5–24.6). Put the clip in a heart mask on a soft white background with an outer glow. Split the mask into two halves on the beat, drift them apart with ease-in-out over ~1.5 s, and build the small tracked text between them. Use it for loss or separation moments.
3. **Flat-colour type card intercut** (6.2, 16.5, 18.8). Use a solid background with one bold type layout and 3D-rotated or slide-in words, held 1–1.5 s. It resets attention between dense footage sections.
4. **Recursive coloured frame zoom-out** (1.6–2.6). Put the clip in a rounded frame and nest it in successive frames. Swap the background colour each beat subdivision and scale down. End with a radial zoom blur cut.
5. **Card carousel memory montage** (4.7–6.1). Rounded, drop-shadowed, sepia clips slide horizontally. Change the background colour per card, and push the last card to full screen.
6. **Stinger ending** (24.8–25.8). Iris to black, then one huge white word that scales up, a 3-frame white flash, and a fade.
