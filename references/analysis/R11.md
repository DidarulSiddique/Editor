## Signature techniques (reusable)
1. **Paper-stage collage world** (whole video): single beige textured background plane; mixed-source cut-outs with soft drop shadows; camera traverses it in long moves. Build in AE/Remotion with 3–5 parallax depths.
2. **Object relay storytelling** (4.25–12.85): each scene's moving object leads the camera to the next (rider → whip → gun → bullet → hand). Ideal for logo/name reveals with no cuts.
3. **Name as world object** (7.3–8.7): place the title on the ground plane at the horizon so it parallaxes with the field; colour it the accent red.
4. **Beat-acting props** (0.25–2.6): animate a prop (drumstick) to strike exactly on hits; the beat is visualised without cuts.
5. **Mono flash block** (0.87–1.47): switch the whole frame to dark monochrome for ~18 f on a hit, then back — a cheap, striking accent.
6. **Echo trails on entrances** (9.5–9.7): 3–4 ghost copies at decreasing opacity for fast arm/object moves.
