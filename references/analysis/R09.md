## Signature techniques (reusable)
1. **Word-tracking camera** (1.0–2.6 s): lay out the full line in one text layer, reveal words on syllable timing, keyframe the camera X so the newest word stays centred; at the line end, ease out to show the whole line. ~4–6 frames per word.
2. **Scene-becomes-post** (2.6–3.4 s): scale the full composite down to ~55 % inside a white card with social icons, then tilt the camera down to new text beneath. Good for "meta"/caption moments.
3. **3D text void** (6.2–10.8 s): white background, thin perspective grid lines, one coloured anchor shape; place words on 3D planes and orbit the camera with roll; drop the subject cut-out through it. Use for lyrics about confusion/falling.
4. **Neon constellation finale** (16.9–21.4 s): each word a separate layer with its own colour + outer glow, rotated −20…+30°, faded in on its syllable, accumulating until the frame is full. Ends a phrase with a "poster" frame.
5. **Prop-based transitions** (13.0 s page curl, 13.6 s marker circle): use a thematic object (calendar for "a year ago") as both transition and canvas.
