## Signature techniques (reusable)
1. **Texture-filled silhouette with echo trails.** At 0–1.4 s: put a character silhouette matte over black and fill it with a pattern plate. Duplicate the layer 2–3 times, offset each copy ~5–10% of frame width up and left, and lower their opacity (≈70/45/25%). Cross-dissolve the fill to a second pattern over ~0.5 s. Use it for intros, character reveals or a cultural motif.
2. **Print-finish stack.** Over the whole edit: a halftone dot overlay, a light paper texture, a vignette and a thin white inset border (~1.5% margin). It turns mixed sources into one "poster" look and suits stills-based or manga edits.
3. **Pull-back reveal on a still collage.** At 1.5–2.8 s: start at ≈1.6x scale on a detail and ease out to 1.0x over ~40 frames. Combine it with a radial white bloom transition in.
4. **Radial white iris transition.** At 1.4 and 8.8 s: a white elliptical gradient grows from the centre over ~3 frames, then dissolves into the next shot. It works as a soft "flash" between still images.
5. **Light-leak flash on an impact clip.** At 4.43 s: one white frame, then an over-exposed warm clip with additive light leaks, landed on the strong hit.
