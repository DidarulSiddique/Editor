## Typography
- **Typeface:** tall, very condensed, hand-drawn sans with rounded stroke ends and uneven letter widths (like a marker or Amatic-style display face). One family throughout, all caps. Probably the same font with a soft outer glow (white, yellow, cyan or red).
- **Scale:** cap height is usually 55–75% of frame height (STOP at 14.1 s ≈ 75%). Single words fill 40–80% of the width. There is no secondary text except the small "@ZENIN" tag at bottom centre (~4% height), which is on every shot.
- **Colour logic:** text colour is chosen from the shot's key light. Yellow on warm shots (2.0, 10.2, 20.3 s), cyan/white on night or blue shots (6.1, 14.0, 26.8 s), red on the fire shot (11.3 s). The words read as light inside the scene, not as captions.

- **Timing:** one word per sung syllable or word. Fast lines change every 3–6 frames (13.0–14.9 s). Held words last 0.6–1.0 s.

## Signature techniques (reusable)
1. **Word-behind-subject lyric type.** Seen at 12.0, 14.1, 15.4, 21.5 and 26.8 s. Layer order: footage, then the giant condensed word (55–75% of frame height) with outer glow, then a rotoscoped subject matte. One word per sung word, cut on the syllable. Suits any lyric or emotional edit with clear foreground subjects.
2. **Scene-lit type colour.** Sample the shot's dominant highlight (yellow, cyan or red) for the fill and add a matching glow so the word reads as diegetic light. Seen at 2.0, 6.1 and 11.3 s.
3. **Planar-tracked word.** At 10.2 s the word "FLOOR" is corner-pinned onto the floor and tracked while feet move over it. Save it for one hero moment per edit.
4. **Mirrored reflection word.** At 6.1 s the word is duplicated, flipped vertically, darkened and placed over the water. Works on any water, glass or polished floor.
5. **Letter-by-letter build with bloom.** At 7.2–7.9 s IGNITES reveals one letter every 2–3 frames under rising glow. Use it for a held, important word.
6. **Motivated FX wipe off the type.** At 28.0 s a smoke or dust plate crosses the frame and erases the word (~8 frames).
7. **Over-exposure flash transition.** At 2.7 and 26.7 s the image ramps to white over 3–5 frames before the next word.

## Weaknesses / don't copy
- Too many white-out and bloom frames. Faces and words wash out (2.7, 26.7, 29.8–32.5 s), and 202 near-white frames is excessive.
- Some words are unreadable (3.5–4.5, 7.9–9.5 s) because they are scribbled or sit on busy backgrounds.
- The hand-drawn condensed face gets thin at small sizes and looks amateurish if overused.
- The persistent "@ZENIN" tag at bottom centre competes with the subjects.
- The source footage is soft and compressed.
