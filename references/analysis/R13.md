## Typography
- **Primary display:** classical roman serif caps (Trajan/Cinzel class), regular weight, very wide tracking (~0.4–0.6 em), cap height ~3–4% of frame height (≈16–20 px on 576). "SPAWN OF THE DEVIL" larger (~6% height) stacked in four lines with a stepped right indent (3.7 s). White with a soft outer glow.
- **Numerals:** the percentages use a different, clean sans (Helvetica/Arial-like, light-regular), ~6–7% frame height, with a smaller "%" glyph (~50% size). This sans/serif mix is the hierarchy device: number = loud, label = quiet.
- **Body copy:** tiny (~2% height) typewriter-style serif/mono with glow and blur, 3 lines ragged-left ("of ELDIANS will be stripped of their ability to reproduce.", 8.2 s). Barely legible at phone size — it reads as texture.

- **Counters:** "THE RUMBLING 41 %→80 %" (7.4–7.9 s) and "64 %→92 %" (8.0–8.4 s) increment several points per frame — a fake data readout.
- **Placement:** in negative space beside the subject (left-top, right-mid, low-centre), anchored to thin vertical/horizontal rules; never over faces. No masking behind subjects observed.

## Signature techniques (reusable)
1. **Assembling glow-serif title on black with a PiP crop** (0.0–0.7 s): black bg, 12–15% wide image card centred with a vertical hairline; serif caps at ~4% height, letters fade in in random order over 0.5 s with 20–30 px glow. Good cold open for "documentary" edits.
2. **Data counter overlay** (7.4–8.4, 14.0–15.9 s): big sans number ticking 2–4 units per frame toward a final value, small "%" glyph, serif label above, tiny 3-line caption, all anchored to a vertical + horizontal hairline. Suits stat reveals (e.g. a player's conversion rate).
3. **Hairline-anchored lower third** (6.5–7.0 s): two 1–2 px glowing rules slide in from opposite edges (ease-out, ~8 frames), name assembles between them.
4. **Redaction split-screen** (9.7–10.9 s): thin vertical rule splits frame; white/black solid rectangles cover parts of the image; small code label ("TS - 13"). "Classified file" vibe.
5. **Invert flash accents**: 1–2 frame negative/greyscale version of the current shot on accents (3.3, 7.1, 7.9 s).
6. **Section break**: 1 white frame + ~0.4 s black (8.5 s).
