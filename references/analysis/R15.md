## Signature techniques (reusable)
1. **Giant word behind subject** (4.6, 13.2, 16–19, 25.4 s): duplicate the clip; roto the subject (or occluding object) to a matte; stack bg plate → word (condensed caps, 45–70% height, centred) → roto subject. Swap word per syllable. Suits name/stat reveals on a player cut-out.
