## Typography
- **This is the identity.** The lyric is staged inside "found" interfaces, so the type borrows each UI's style.
- **Logotype** (0.1–2.5): a heavy geometric sans with a hard-offset dark-green extrusion, matching the illustration's palette. "RAP" ≈25% of frame height when close.
- **Caption UI** (2.7–3.4): bold rounded geometric sans (like Gilroy/Poppins Bold), ≈8% of frame height. The secondary line is grey.
- **Ampersand** (3.4): oversized ≈30% of frame height, with an outline stroke in the accent green.
- **Label text** (3.5–4.75): a distressed/irregular display face set in rectangular label boxes, like a ransom-note or tape-label collage.
- **UI text** (4.8–7.35): the interface's own type, enlarged until it reads as a headline.
- **Correction gag** (5.8–6.5): hand-drawn scribble strike-through. The lowercase italic-feel sans reads like a comment.
- **Ending** (8.3–10.1):
  - Physics-scattered rounded lowercase ("fuck around").
  - Word-boxed labels in a monospace-like sans, alternating white box/black text and green box/white text.
- **Hierarchy:** one phrase on screen at a time, so it is always readable.
- **Placement:** follows the UI's logic (caption bar, comment line, calculator display).
- **Timing:** type-on syncs to vocal syllables, not only beats. Most rhythmic events land near hits (0.05, 3.51, 8.54).

## Signature techniques (reusable)
1. **Lyric staged inside a found UI** (4.8–8.9). Recreate a phone screen (post, share sheet, calculator) and make the lyric its native text. Enlarge it, then glide a tilted 3D camera across. It works for stats or quotes presented as a "feed".
2. **Strike-through correction gag** (5.8–6.5). Show the misheard or wrong word, animate a hand-drawn zig-zag scribble over it (≈6 frames), then type the right word beneath.
3. **Motivated UI-to-UI links** (6.6–7.5). A small element in scene A (the Calculator icon) becomes the setting of scene B. Connect them with a ≈4-frame blur dissolve.
4. **Ransom-label word boxes** (3.5, 8.3–8.9). Each word sits in its own filled rectangle with the accent colour alternating. Words pop in one per syllable with small offsets for a hand-made feel.
5. **Physics letter spill** (8.3–8.9). After a phrase is read, its letters drop and scatter onto UI objects below, freeing space for the next line.
6. **Logotype assemble from the art's palette** (0.1–0.4). Title letters fly in rotating with blur and overshoot into place, extruded in the illustration's accent colour.
